> **Faction:** Clubs (♣️) | **Role:** Energy Battery / Armor Debuffer / Attack Speed Buffer

> **F2P Rec:** Base dupe early → Divine

### Why Diana Is Underrated

The Excel and CN both rate Diana T2, which is correct in a vacuum. But the EG endgame player makes a specific case: **if you have Cancer and Spade units, Diana becomes A-tier**. The reason is Crescent's Mark — her armor debuff is **the second-strongest controllable armor debuff in the game**, and due to how armor calculation works in Motto Immortal, armor reduction has disproportionate damage scaling effects.

Most players see "attack speed buff + energy battery" and think support filler. The armor debuff mechanic is what makes her genuinely impactful.

### Key Synergies

**Diana + Zeus/Nezha:** Her attack speed buff directly benefits Zeus's judgment window. Late-game, she's described as a "Zeus/Nezha pet."
**Diana + Cancer + Spade units:** EG condition for A-tier. The armor debuff + Cancer's super armor + Spade AoE carries = multiplicative damage amplification.
