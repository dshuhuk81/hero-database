## Pan (木菩)

> **Faction:** Clubs (♣️) | **Role:** CC / Energy Drain / Control
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** A
> **Relic Priority:** r0 (functional) → r20 (ideal)
> **F2P Rec:** Base dupe early → Divine

### Why Pan Is The Best CC Unit In The Game

Pan's kit is entirely CC: ultimate clears all enemy energy, minor skills apply silence + fear + sleep. CN calls him "控到死" (CC to death) — enemies literally cannot use their ultimates if Pan is active. The EG confirms r20 is "working and insane."

The Excel rates him S PvE / A PvP, CN rates T0.5 in faction. The EG doesn't directly contradict either — just confirms r20 is the ideal stopping point. The r0 baseline is enough to handle most Odyssey matchups and specific counter-picks.

### Core Mechanic: Energy Removal

Energy removal is rare in this game. Most CC units stun or push — Pan's ultimate removes energy entirely, resetting the enemy team's ultimate cycle. Against teams that depend on fast ultimate rotations (Caishen-boosted teams, Zeus), this is a hard counter that can't be played around.
