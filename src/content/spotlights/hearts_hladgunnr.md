## Hladgunnr (荷拉德古娜)

> **Faction:** Hearts (♥️) | **Role:** Frontline Tank / Warrior
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** C (Excel) / B+–A- (EG) | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r20–r30
> **F2P Rec:** Divine → D5 (dupes over time)

### Core Mechanic

Hladgunnr is a tanky frontline warrior with super armor at r30, making her effectively unkillable in most scenarios. Her PvE rating is debated: the Excel rates her C, the CN list rates her T10086 in her faction (very low), but the EG endgame player pushes back strongly, rating her **B+/A- for PvE**. The disconnect comes from a specific mechanic: at high investment, Hladgunnr's frontline **cannot be broken**, meaning enemies that rely on bypassing or repositioning around the frontline are countered entirely.

### The EG Disagreement

The EG player's argument: stat dampening currently prevents Hladgunnr from shining in the tier list context, but with more carries available in the meta, she can be used to **exploit specific stage layouts** where her unbreakable line generates massive value. This is a situational strength that tier lists don't capture well.

For Heart Spire specifically, her tankiness is the main draw. For general Odyssey, she's niche.
