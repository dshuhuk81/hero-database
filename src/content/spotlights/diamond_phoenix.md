## Phoenix (菲尼克斯)

> **Faction:** Diamond (♦️) | **Role:** DPS / Sub-Carry
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** A | **PvP:** A
> **Relic Priority:** r20
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Core Mechanic

Phoenix has the "infinite lives" mechanic — she revives on death, giving her sustained presence through long fights. Early in the game she's a primary DPS; later she becomes a sub-DPS alongside Hercules. CN notes the revive makes her "good damage early on, sub-DPS later with infinite lives."
