## Yanluo (阎罗王)

> **Faction:** Hearts (♥️) | **Role:** CC / Tech / Displacement
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** B | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r10 (Nezha synergy) → r20 (full tech)
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Core Mechanic

Yanluo forcibly pulls a specific enemy to him at fight start and suppresses them for 6 seconds. Enemies who attack Yanluo lose energy. His ultimate is a high-multiplier execution skill.

This makes him the premier **counter-pick unit** — if the enemy has a fast-start carry or energy-hungry support, Yanluo neutralizes them at fight start. CN describes him as "永远T0" (eternally T0) for this specific use case, with the caveat that he's used when necessary, not universally.

### EG Insight: More Tech Than You Think

The EG player elevates Yanluo significantly beyond his tier list placement:
- **r10 + Nezha:** The pull timing lines up with Nezha's jump kick. The +20% ATK buff from Yanluo's skill stacks on top of Nezha's output. This is not a coincidence — it's a deliberate sync point that requires knowing exactly when Nezha jumps.
- **r20:** The suppress duration and energy drain become powerful enough to **prevent units like Ra from being annoying** — a specific anti-meta application that matters in PvP.

The EG player is clear: "You can use him just for the pull, but Yan has a ton of tech."
