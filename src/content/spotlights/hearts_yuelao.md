## Yuelao (月老)

> **Faction:** Hearts (♥️) | **Role:** Buffer / Healer / Support
> **Overall Tier:** T0 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r1–r10 early → r20–r30 advanced
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Core Mechanic

Yuelao is the only unit in the game that creates a **stat-sharing link** between your highest-ATK unit and your highest-HP unit. Linked units share 5% of each other's HP, ATK, armor, and magic resist. His other skills heal the lowest-HP linked ally and restore 150 energy to the highest-ATK ally with a +15% ATK buff for 8 seconds.

This means Yuelao functions as both a healer and a buffer simultaneously — roles that are normally separate. The link mechanic is what separates him from generic supports.

### Why He's Tricky (EG Insight)

The EG endgame player notes that Yuelao's value is highly context-dependent:
- **r10 (ideal baseline):** The link and energy restoration are enough for most standard use cases.
- **r30 (Hecate tech + Bossing):** At r30, certain synergies involving Hecate and boss fights make the relic gains substantial — specifically, r30 in some scenarios creates **permanent survival** for your carry through sustained heals and buffing. This was described as something that "will come to light soon" suggesting ongoing meta development.

No Yuelao breakpoint is "bad" — all of them add value. The question is where he fits in your investment sequence.

### Key Synergies

**Yuelao + Hecate + Momus:** The standard Spade carry trio. Yuelao links Hecate (highest ATK) with Momus (highest HP frontline), creating a loop where both units share defensive stats and Hecate gets sustained energy/ATK buffs.
**Yuelao + Meng Po + Caishen:** The Hearts core trio. Yuelao's heals and ATK buffs keep carries alive and outputting.
