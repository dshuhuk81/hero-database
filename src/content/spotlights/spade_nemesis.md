## Nemesis (涅墨西斯)

> **Faction:** Spade (♠️) | **Role:** Early Carry / PvP
> **Overall Tier:** T10 | **PvE:** C | **PvP:** A
> **Relic Priority:** r0
> **F2P Rec:** Divine (after rest done)

### Notes

Nemesis carries early PvP and falls off hard as DPS. Her damage is too random and slow for consistent late-game performance. The PvP A-rating comes from her early-game burst window, not sustained performance. CN describes her as "Nyx's inferior."
