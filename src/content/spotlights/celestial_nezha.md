## Nezha (哪吒)

> **Faction:** Celestial (⭐) | **Role:** PvE DPS Carry
> **Overall Tier:** T0 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r30–r50
> **F2P Rec:** Divine (secondary Celestial after Zeus)

### Core Mechanic

Nezha is the strongest PvE DPS carry in the game. At divine, he can clear most Odyssey teams with 4 level-1 support units. His kit is boss-agnostic and PvE-optimized. Unlike Zeus who is universally strong, Nezha peaks in PvE — his PvP performance is S on paper but Zeus is more impactful in competitive PvP.

CN notes: without Totem system active, he needs 4 supports; with Totem, those 4 supports are redundant.

**Awakened gear: Slots 1/3/4 (same as Zeus — share where possible).**

### Yanluo Synergy (EG)

Yanluo's r10 timing lines up with Nezha's jump kick, delivering +20% ATK on contact. This is the specific Yanluo tech the EG highlighted most strongly.
