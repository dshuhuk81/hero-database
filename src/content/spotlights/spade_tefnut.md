## Tefnut (忒芙努特)

> **Faction:** Spade (♠️) | **Role:** Support / Cleanser / Damage Mitigation
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** A | **PvP:** A
> **Relic Priority:** r10 early → r20 late
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Why Tefnut Matters More Than She Appears

Both the Excel and CN rate Tefnut T1 A/A — accurate but missing the most important detail. The EG endgame player's key addition: **Tefnut is the only pure cleanser in the game.** No other unit removes debuffs from allies. In fights where the enemy team applies persistent debuffs (slows, ATK reduction, CC), Tefnut's cleanse is the only answer.

Additionally she provides 50% AoE damage reduction, which is an enormous defensive multiplier for your entire team during grouped fights (i.e., after Poseidon's pull).

### Key Synergies

**Tefnut + Poseidon:** The 50% AoE damage reduction is maximized when all enemies are clustered — Poseidon creates that cluster, Tefnut absorbs the incoming burst.
**Tefnut + any team vs. debuff-heavy enemies:** Her cleanse is uniquely valuable in specific matchups.
