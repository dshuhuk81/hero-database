## Amun (阿蒙·拉)

> **Faction:** Celestial (⭐) | **Role:** Grouper / Frontline
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** A | **PvP:** A
> **Relic Priority:** r30
> **F2P Rec:** Free unit

### Core Mechanic

"Deluxe edition Poseidon" for PvE — better grouping tool but at higher investment cost. Amun is the second-worst Celestial to invest in per the Excel. His dodge-stacking mechanic is strong but requires high investment to shine. Most accounts don't need him to progress, but he's a free unit so Divine is achievable without targeted pulls.
