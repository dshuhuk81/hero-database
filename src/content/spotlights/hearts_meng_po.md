## Meng Po (孟婆) — 女武神 / Valkyrie

> **Faction:** Hearts (♥️) | **Role:** Support / Tank / Carry
> **Overall Tier:** T0 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r1 early → r20 mid → r30 late
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Why Meng Po Is Misunderstood

On paper she is labeled a support. In practice she is the most complete unit in the game — CN sources describe her kit as having "almost no weakness." The issue is that most players either over-invest early (rushing r30 when r1 is enough to function) or under-invest late (stopping at r20 when r30 unlocks her as a solo carry threat).

### Core Mechanic

Meng Po combines three things no other unit does simultaneously: infinite revives for teammates, a personal untargetable window during her ultimate, and an energy battery role. The revive mechanic keeps your entire team alive through high-pressure stages; the untargetable window makes her essentially unkillable while she's outputting; the energy generation feeds your carries continuously.

**⚠️ HA (Hero Arena) Bug:** Revives triggered by Meng Po do not carry over to the next battle in Hero Arena. This does not affect her overall tier but is critical to know for HA-specific strategy.

### Key Synergies

**Meng Po + Caishen:** Caishen's energy battery accelerates Meng Po's ultimate cycle, increasing revive and untargetable uptime.
**Meng Po + Jingwei:** Jingwei borrows Zeus gear and benefits from Meng Po's revive safety net. Meng Po lets Jingwei play aggressively without fatal risk.
**Meng Po + Yuelao:** The Hearts core trio. Yuelao's buff links + Meng Po's revives + Caishen's energy = a self-sustaining loop that is extremely hard to break in PvP.
