## Momus (摩摩斯)

> **Faction:** Spade (♠️) | **Role:** Frontline Tank / Shielder / Carry Enabler
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** A
> **Relic Priority:** r1–r10 early → r20 late
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Why Momus Is Deeper Than He Looks

The Excel rates him T1 S/A, which is accurate but undersells the kit depth. The EG endgame player's key insight: **Momus's shields have no global cooldown (GCD)**. At 25% shield generation, this creates a situation where shields are being applied almost continuously during extended fights, especially in PvP. "Supermassive" was the EG's description.

Additionally, his stats directly mirror Hecate's — CN notes "his stats = Hecate stats." The Yuelao link chains them: what Hecate gains, Momus gains, and vice versa.

### Key Synergies

**Momus + Hecate + Yuelao:** The Spade core trio. Momus tanks and generates shields; Yuelao links him to Hecate; Hecate outputs.
**Momus + Poseidon:** Poseidon groups enemies; Momus taunts the cluster; carries output.
**Momus + Skadi:** Taunt forces enemies to attack into Skadi's AoE.
