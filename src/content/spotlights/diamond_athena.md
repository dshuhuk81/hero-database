## Athena (雅典娜)

> **Faction:** Diamond (♦️) | **Role:** Zeus Buffer / Attack Speed Support
> **Overall Tier:** T2 | **PvE:** B | **PvP:** B
> **Relic Priority:** r0 (base) → r20 (only if Divine Zeus)
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Core Mechanic

Athena provides a high attack speed buff that specifically benefits Zeus's judgment window. She has hidden depth similar to Khepri — her kit does many things, but her primary identity is enabling Zeus. **Only invest to r20 if you have Divine Zeus.** Before that, she contributes very little. CN confirms: "Only invest in relic 20 if you have divine Zeus."
