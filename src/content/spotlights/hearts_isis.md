## Isis (伊西斯)

> **Faction:** Hearts (♥️) | **Role:** DPS / Boss
> **Overall Tier:** T10 | **PvE:** C | **PvP:** C
> **Relic Priority:** r0
> **F2P Rec:** Can rush Divine for early stat padding only

### Core Mechanic

Isis has max HP% damage + armor penetration + crit buffs during her ultimate, making her theoretically excellent against tanky bosses. She's the second-best single-target bossing unit after Zeus. **In practice:** she's a free unit (given as a 7-day login reward), which is the only reason to use her. CN and the Excel agree she's easily replaceable and a troll investment for both F2P and whales at any real resource depth.
