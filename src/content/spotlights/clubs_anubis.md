## Anubis (阿努比斯)

> **Faction:** Clubs (♣️) | **Role:** Assassin / Execute / Tank
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** A | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r20
> **F2P Rec:** Divine → D5

### Core Mechanic

Anubis has three defining mechanics working together: vampiric healing, an untargetable window when he has lost 40% max HP cumulatively, and an execute threshold (at r20: kills enemies below 5% HP even through invincible skills). The execute cuts through mechanics like Ares's 5-second invincibility — this is why CN calls him "一闪一闪亮晶晶，挨个排队杀干净" (kill them one by one in sequence). The untargetable window means he can survive his own aggression.
