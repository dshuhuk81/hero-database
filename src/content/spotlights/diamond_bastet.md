## Bastet (芭丝特)

> **Faction:** Diamond (♦️) | **Role:** Frontline Tank / Solo Carry
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** A | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r20 (tank) → r30 (DPS)
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Core Mechanic

Bastet is the recommended frontline unit for the Diamond faction. She can solo-carry up until the 5-team threshold — meaning early-to-mid-game accounts can run her as both their frontline and primary damage source. At higher investment she transitions into a dedicated frontline with Hercules or Athena as the carry.

Key gear note: she needs **dodge% on slot 2/3** to function properly. Without dodge investment, her survivability drops significantly.
