## Prometheus (普罗米修斯)

> **Faction:** Hearts (♥️) | **Role:** Frontline Tank
> **Overall Tier:** T2 (Excel) / T1 (CN) | **PvE:** B | **PvP:** B
> **Relic Priority:** r1 (unlock only)
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Core Mechanic

Prometheus is a frontline tank whose cage-drop passive is the primary reason to invest. The cage traps an enemy in place, creating positioning opportunities. He's a tech unit — used for specific stages where his pushback or aggro-taking ability solves a problem that tankier options can't.

CN rates him slightly higher (T1) than the Excel (T2/B), describing him as "the first properly useful helper" at his investment level. For most accounts, r1 is the stopping point — you unlock the relic for the cage effect and stop there.
