## Zeus (宙斯)

> **Faction:** Celestial (⭐) | **Role:** Universal Carry
> **Overall Tier:** T0 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r30–r50
> **F2P Rec:** Divine (primary F2P Celestial target)

### Core Mechanic

Zeus is the most versatile unit in the game — he functions in every mode, every team composition, and at every stage of progression. His ultimate (Judgment) delivers a massive burst damage window that is buffed by Athena's attack speed, Yuelao's ATK buff, and Dionysus's damage boost. At Divine, he can clear most Odyssey stages with 4 level-1 support units.

**F2P Route:** The standard advice is to invest everything into Zeus until Divine, then shift to Nezha. You can get 5–6 Zeus copies in 61 days through the Mindsea shop on the Zeus route. Awakened gear: slots 1/3/4.

### Key Synergies

**Zeus + Athena:** Attack speed buff directly amplifies Judgment window.
**Zeus + Yuelao:** ATK buff + link chain = Zeus hits harder and survives longer.
**Zeus + Caishen + Dionysus:** Maximum energy cycling. Zeus + full support stack is the endgame meta team.
**Zeus + Diana:** Diana's armor debuff + Zeus's Judgment = disproportionate burst damage.
