## Nyx (倪克斯)

> **Faction:** Celestial (⭐) | **Role:** PvP Assassin / Early PvE Carry
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** A | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r30 (general) → r50 (PvP)
> **F2P Rec:** Base dupe → wait for random drop

### Core Mechanic

Nyx is the most broken PvP unit in the game. She hard-carries early PvE but falls off in late-game PvE and transitions to sub-DPS/tech. Her assassin mechanic (diagonal traversal, untargetable during execution) means she cuts through frontlines to reach priority targets.

CN describes her as "断档领先，平民战神" (tier above the rest, F2P war god) — one star is enough to use her immediately.
