## Jingwei (精卫)

> **Faction:** Hearts (♥️) | **Role:** Ranged Carry / Assassin
> **Overall Tier:** T0.5–T1 | **PvE:** A+ | **PvP:** A+
> **Relic Priority:** r10–r20 (Zeus route) or r20–r30 (standalone)
> **F2P Rec:** Divine → D5 (dupes over time)

### Core Mechanic

Jingwei is a ranged carry who jumps to the back row, marks the farthest enemy for +20% incoming damage, then enters a persistent flight phase where she fires wind blades every 1.5 seconds at the most distant target. Her damage scales with distance — maximum amplification kicks in beyond 11 meters, giving her a +20% damage bonus at peak range.

This makes her unique: she doesn't just deal damage, she punishes enemies who try to keep distance from her, and she specifically targets the back row (enemy supports and carries) rather than frontline tanks.

### Why She's The F2P Carry

Every other standard (non-Celestial) carry has a clear weakness or requires specific team compositions. Jingwei can carry a team solo from standard investment up to T4 gear. CN notes she remains viable "up until late game from standard units." The key point for F2P: **she shares Zeus's awakened gear slots (1/3/4)**, meaning if you're building Zeus anyway, Jingwei gets her gear for free.

**EG note:** Yanluo at r10 lines up with Jingwei's jump kick to deliver +20% ATK on top of her existing mark. This is a meaningful damage multiplier.

### Key Synergies

**Jingwei + Caishen:** Energy cycling feeds her flight phase. The longer she stays airborne, the more damage she accumulates.
**Jingwei + Meng Po:** Revives give Jingwei a second life if she gets focused while airborne.
**Jingwei + Yanluo r10:** Yanluo's +20% ATK buff syncs with Jingwei's jump to create a burst window.
