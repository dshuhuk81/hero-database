## Nuwa (女娲)

> **Faction:** Celestial (⭐) | **Role:** Best Tank / CC / Support
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** up to r30
> **F2P Rec:** Base dupe → wait, or grab from Mindsea

### Core Mechanic

Nuwa is the best tank in the game. She can perma-CC enemies, share damage between allied units (reducing burst kill potential), and perma-CC enemies simultaneously. All of this works at base dupe — she's one of the best base-dupe value units in the game.

**Mindsea note:** Only Nuwa is worth grabbing as a base copy from the Mindsea shop if you're unlucky with pulls.

Post-Totem: she locks in Dionysus and Hera as team synergies, plus Athena and Moon Goddess pairs.
