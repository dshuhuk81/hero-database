## Skadi (斯卡蒂)

> **Faction:** Spade (♠️) | **Role:** Hard Carry / AoE DPS
> **Overall Tier:** T0 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r10 early → r20 mid → r30–r35 late
> **F2P Rec:** Divine → D5 (dupes over time)

### Core Mechanic

Skadi is the closest non-Celestial unit to a Celestial carry. Her AoE damage scales with enemy density, she has built-in lifesteal, and she doesn't contest any other meta team's carries — meaning she slots into any comp without disrupting existing synergies. CN calls her the "closest DPS to a Celestial we have in game."

Her range of use is extraordinary: she clears most enemy teams in Odyssey, dominates PvP at S tier, and performs in Spire. The only constraint is she needs Poseidon's grouping to hit peak efficiency.

### Key Synergies

**Skadi + Poseidon:** The foundational pairing. Poseidon groups; Skadi's AoE hits the entire cluster at maximum efficiency.
**Skadi + Momus:** Momus taunts into Skadi's AoE. Enemies are pulled to a point, taunted in place, and then deleted.
**Skadi + Yuelao + Hecate:** The full Spade meta trio. Skadi as secondary carry, Hecate as primary, Yuelao buffing both.
