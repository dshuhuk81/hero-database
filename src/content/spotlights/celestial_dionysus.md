## Dionysus (狄奥尼索斯 / 酒神)

> **Faction:** Celestial (⭐) | **Role:** DPS-Boost Support / Energy Battery
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** A+ | **PvP:** A
> **Relic Priority:** r1 or r30
> **F2P Rec:** Base dupe → wait for random drop

### Core Mechanic

One of the few energy batteries in the game, with insane damage-boosting buffs. At full uptime, she's the highest DPS-increasing support in the game. The weakness: survival issues. If she dies early, her buff window disappears. CN describes it as: "If you can keep her alive, it's the highest DPS-increasing support currently."

The r1 vs r30 split: r1 is enough for energy battery function; r30 unlocks the full buff potential. F2P accounts can often run r1 and supplement with positioning to keep her alive.
