### The Hela Controversy

CN and Excel agree: T10/C/B. Skadi does everything Hela does but infinitely better. However, the EG endgame player **explicitly and vehemently contests the D-tier placement**, taking it as a personal attack.

The theoretical case for Hela: her chain-kill mechanic can theoretically loop through weak targets after Poseidon clusters them — each kill enables another jump, potentially clearing the entire cluster. At high investment with Poseidon grouping, this could be more impactful than the tier lists suggest. However, this case is not well-documented in CN sources, and Skadi's passive AoE lifesteal approach is simply more reliable.

**Confidence: Low on Hela upside. The EG defense is noted but unverified.**
