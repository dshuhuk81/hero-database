## Chronos (时间神 / 柯罗诺斯)

> **Faction:** Celestial (⭐) | **Role:** Universal Support
> **Overall Tier:** T0 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r30
> **F2P Rec:** Base dupe → work on dupes after Zeus/Nezha

### Core Mechanic

Chronos is described as "the best support in the game — does everything." Complete mechanic, complete CC, complete support, full-map employment. His full kit is largely unlocked at base dupe, making him extremely F2P-friendly.

CN notes one important caveat: if his ultimate is fully charged and then delayed, the release needs precise timing — too much charge and the delayed cast can be awkward to control.
