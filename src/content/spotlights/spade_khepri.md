### Core Mechanic

Khepri is a push-and-group tech unit used alongside Poseidon. At r1 he unlocks his core mechanic; his tankiness scales with relic investment. The EG player notes that Khepri, like Athena, has **"so many things"** — a kit with more depth than his tier listing suggests. Specific mechanics are not fully documented in available sources.
