## Hecate (赫卡忒)

> **Faction:** Spade (♠️) | **Role:** Primary Carry / Sustained AoE
> **Overall Tier:** T0.5–T1 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** A
> **Relic Priority:** r20 early → r30 late
> **F2P Rec:** Divine → D5

### Core Mechanic

Hecate's ultimate is a 10-second sustained damage channel with the highest total damage multiplier in the game. The problem: it outputs slowly. This creates her core tension — in early PvE stages, fast burst from Fengyi/Nuba clears enemies before Hecate's damage even registers. In late-game content, her total output becomes unmatched.

Her meta role: she forms the core trio with **Yuelao + Momus**. Yuelao links Hecate (highest ATK) and Momus (highest HP), sharing stats and providing sustained ATK buffs. Momus's shields keep Hecate alive during her 10-second channel.

### Why PvP Is Lower

Hecate's slow damage output means PvP fights can end before she completes her channel. She's A-tier PvP rather than S because of this, despite being S-tier PvE.
