## Hercules (赫拉克勒斯)

> **Faction:** Diamond (♦️) | **Role:** Hard Carry / Frontline AoE
> **Overall Tier:** T0 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r20 early → r30 mid → r50 (if possible)
> **F2P Rec:** Divine → D5

### Core Mechanic

Hercules is the Diamond faction's answer to Skadi — a hard carry who groups enemies with his charge and then delivers two massive burst damage windows. His damage pattern is: pull/group → first burst → second burst. The gap between the two bursts is the window for the enemy team to respond; at high investment, the second burst arrives before they can recover.

CN and Excel are completely aligned: T0, S/S. The main warning from both: **avoid using him against Pan** — Pan's energy drain neutralizes his burst windows.

### Key Synergies

**Hercules + Athena:** Athena's attack speed buff benefits Hercules's judgment window (same principle as with Zeus). At divine Zeus investment level.
**Hercules + Poseidon (cross-faction):** Poseidon groups; Hercules bursts the cluster. Cross-faction but highly effective.
**Hercules gear: Slots 2/4.** Priority awakening slots per the Excel.
