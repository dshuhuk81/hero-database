## Cancer (巨蟹座)

> **Faction:** Hearts (♥️) | **Role:** Energy Battery / CC / Frontline
> **Overall Tier:** T0.5–T1 | **PvE:** A+ | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r1 early → r20 late
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Core Mechanic

Cancer is the only useful Zodiac unit in the game. She provides energy to frontline allies, applies super armor (crowd control immunity), and is one of only a few energy batteries in the game outside Caishen. Her PvP tier is higher than her PvE tier because super armor and energy feeding in a 5v5 format is disproportionately valuable — it means your frontline can't be CC'd out of their skills during fight initiation.

### EG Perspective

The EG player rates Cancer as A-tier PvP (rather than S), noting that S is reserved for "holy grail investment/usage" units. The difference is philosophical — Cancer is strong, but not in the same conversation as Meng Po, Caishen, or Yuelao for raw priority. Practically, the gap between CN's S and EG's A is narrow.

### Key Synergies

**Cancer + Yanluo:** EG treats these as a natural pair — both provide energy and CC utility to frontline. Together they keep energy flowing and prevent the frontline from being CC-locked.
**Cancer + Meng Po:** Super armor helps Meng Po's frontline survive long enough to proc her revive mechanic.
