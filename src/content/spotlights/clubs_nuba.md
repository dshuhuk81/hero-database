## Nuba (大蛇 / 女魃)

> **Faction:** Clubs (♣️) | **Role:** Off-Tank / Sub-DPS / Utility
> **Overall Tier:** T1 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r10–r20 early → r20–r30 late
> **F2P Rec:** Divine → D5

### Why Nuba Is Stronger Than Her Tier Suggests

The Excel rates her T1 with S/S, which already sounds strong — but the EG endgame player is more emphatic: "You're smoking something good if you don't think she's S-tier in PvP." The distinction is that Nuba has **a large number of tricks** that standard tier lists don't capture, and her PvP toolkit depth rivals T0 units in specific matchups.

CN notes that without relic investment, Nuba gives enemies free energy — the inverse of what you want. This is the only real weakness, and it's purely an investment threshold issue.

### Core Mechanic

Nuba is an off-tank sub-DPS with substantial utility: multiple forms of CC, crowd control synergies with the Clubs "big snake" (大蛇) mechanic, and a kit that rewards deep knowledge of positioning. She doesn't just deal damage — she disrupts enemy positioning and creates follow-up opportunities for teammates.

### Key Synergies

**Nuba + Poseidon (cross-faction):** Poseidon groups enemies; Nuba's AoE utility hits the entire cluster. Despite being different factions, they work together effectively.
**Nuba + Pan:** Pan's CC clears energy; Nuba's CC stacks on top. Double CC Clubs composition.
