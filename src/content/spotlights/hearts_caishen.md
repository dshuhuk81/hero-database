## Caishen (财神)

> **Faction:** Hearts (♥️) | **Role:** Support / Energy Battery
> **Overall Tier:** T0 | **PvE:** S | **PvP:** S
> **Relic Priority:** r1 early → r20 mid
> **F2P Rec:** Divine

### Core Mechanic

Caishen is the game's best energy battery, period. His passive generates gold ingots that heal nearby allies below 50% HP, but his defining function is his ultimate: instant 100 energy to all nearby allies + 25 energy/second for 8 seconds. This directly enables your carries to cycle ultimates faster than the enemy can respond.

CN sources describe him as the "eternal support" — the carries change with every meta shift, but Caishen always has a job.

### Why He's T0 Not Just "Good"

Energy management is the core game mechanic of Motto Immortal. Every carry depends on ultimate cycles for their damage output. Caishen accelerates those cycles for the entire team simultaneously. Without him, your carries are slower; with him, they outpace enemy teams. This isn't a situational benefit — it applies in every mode.

### Key Synergies

**Caishen + Jingwei / Isis:** Both carries depend on sustaining their ultimate rotation. Caishen directly enables both. CN notes that Jingwei + Isis + Caishen is a foundational Hearts team for this reason.
**Caishen + Meng Po + Yuelao:** The standard Hearts trio core. All three benefit each other in a closed loop.
**Caishen + Zeus:** CN top-tier endgame use. Zeus's ultimate judgement window benefits enormously from guaranteed energy cycling.
