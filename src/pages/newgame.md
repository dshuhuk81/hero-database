New Android Game:

Gatcha Game

The game is about heroes that are thriving together through a pve campaign mode, where they are about to do fights and conquer stage after stage. The main focus should be pve driven.
Each composition for a fight is consisting of 5 heroes. These formations could be variied but the default composition would be two heroes in front and three at the back.

At the start of the game, you will be guided through some story mode driven stages that explain the game design and how to use your characters in order to win a fight. Those fights are done with one hero at the start and max up until you get 5 heroes.

The skills of the heroes are activated by the player or if set, automatically when the energy is full for a skill to strike.
By then every hero has a default attack.

Heroes can be gained through summoning. With that there should be a ressource that players gain through a) afking b) playing the campaign or by solving events/puzzles. There is no pay shop involved.

What is needed:

1) a backgroind for the game, a settting and theme
2) heroes that fit into that and vary in class, faction.
3) each hero needs indivdual skills that are depending on the class
4) a stage or world design that can be conquered by the player stage after stage
5) a cpu hero boss design that consists of enemies that a player has to defeat. those enemies grow stronger and stronger so that the player has to develop his heroes
6) a hero skill, stat and item system - so that the heroes can grow stronger
7) a level system for each hero - and a hero development system, so that e.g. with multiple heroes, those can be combined
8) a summoning system, where you can do summons (single and a bunch of 10 summons) where cards are released, either they get good or mediocre heroes from that
9) a system for heroes that define their rarity. ranging from common, rare, epic, legendary and mythic heroes. the higher the rarity, the stronger heroes are.
10) base stats should be developed for each hero, that have effect on the battle. Those could be default attack, hp, dodge, defense, crit %, crit DMG, and more that need to be defined.
11) a game engine that allows rapid prototyping and fast development.
12) a fight system

The current base game setup should consist of two things. pve map -> campaign and summoning.
