Nuba

"The world fears, banishes, and curses me. But I just don't understand what I've done wrong."

⚔ Club Faction 🛡 Warrior Class
Overview

Nuba, the Goddess of Drought, is a Warrior of the Club faction whose entire kit revolves around a unique debuff called Crimson Doom Mark. She is a sustained-pressure hybrid — part debuffer, part disabler, part self-sustaining frontliner — who works by marking enemies, draining their resources, and punishing them for carrying her mark.

Unlike most Warriors who deal straightforward burst damage, Nuba layers multiple stacking debuffs over time while simultaneously reducing enemy effectiveness and amplifying her own follow-up damage. Her passive resurrection mechanic via Rise of the Drought gives her unexpected survivability in extended fights.

As a Club faction hero, Nuba scales strongly when fielded alongside other Club heroes — the faction bonus grants stacking ATK and HP to the whole team, reinforcing her preferred role as a durable front-row anchor.

Club Faction Synergy
Faction Bonus — Club

Fielding multiple Club faction heroes grants the entire team bonus ATK and HP. Thresholds:

  • 3 Club heroes: +15% ATK & HP for the team
  • 3 + 2 (mixed): +25% ATK & HP for the team
  • 4 Club heroes: +30% ATK & HP for the team
  • 5 Club heroes: +35% ATK & HP for the team — maximum bonus

Because Nuba benefits from sustained HP (enabling her revive to matter more) and amplified ATK (making her Stun windows hit harder), maximizing the Club faction count directly strengthens her kit. Even hitting the 3-hero threshold for +15% is a meaningful upgrade — running 4–5 Club heroes is a viable dedicated archetype.

Core Mechanic — Crimson Doom Mark
The Doom Mark System
  • Applied to enemies at the start of battle passively via her Ultimate passive, and re-applied to the nearest enemy whenever the Ultimate activates.
  • Marked enemies deal reduced damage — confirmed by both Curse of Decay and Rite of Ashes.
  • When a Doom Mark expires naturally ("falls"), it drains the target's essence and spawns a Source of the Drought Devil at the target's location.
  • Every time Nuba casts her Ultimate, all marked enemies permanently lose ATK SPD — this effect stacks with each Ultimate cast.
  • Skill 1 (Infernal Soulbreaker) deals bonus damage and Stuns targets that carry the mark.
  • The Source of the Drought Devil serves as a revive token — consumed by Rise of the Drought to bring Nuba back to life at her current position.
Skill Breakdown
Siphon of Souls Ultimate

Passive component: At the start of battle, inflicts Crimson Doom Mark on enemies, dealing damage to the host and destroying their Energy. When a Doom Mark expires, it drains the target's essence and transforms into a Source of the Drought Devil at the target's location.

Active component: Upon activation, inflicts Crimson Doom Mark on the nearest enemy, disabling them, dealing damage, and absorbing their Energy.

Dual-purpose: passive mark spread at battle start + active single-target mark, disable, and Energy drain. Energy context: all heroes fill their Energy bar passively at the start of a fight — when full, the Ultimate fires automatically. Nuba's Ultimate both destroys enemy Energy (resetting or delaying their next cast) and absorbs it (potentially accelerating her own refill). This directly delays how fast enemies can cast their own Ultimates. Other heroes, items, or skills can speed up or slow down Energy fill rate — Nuba's drain compounds any existing slow-fill debuffs on enemies.
Curse of Decay Relic

Enemies infested with Crimson Doom Mark deal less DMG. Each time Nuba casts her Ultimate, all enemies infested with Crimson Doom Mark permanently lose ATK SPD.

Relic passive — always active once unlocked. The permanent ATK SPD reduction stacks across every Ultimate cast. Based on available information this reduction does not appear to have diminishing returns, meaning repeated Ultimate casts could theoretically reduce enemy attack speed to near-zero in a long fight. This makes her exponentially more dangerous the more times her Energy fills — which she also accelerates via Energy absorption on her active.
Infernal Soulbreaker Skill 1

Deal damage to an enemy and interrupt them. If the target has Crimson Doom Mark, the skill deals higher damage and Stuns the target instead of simply interrupting.

Primary CC tool. Needs a marked target to reach full potential — which is guaranteed in virtually every fight thanks to her Ultimate passive. Reliable Stun makes this excellent for both PVE bosses and PVP targets.
Rise of the Drought Skill 2

Lv4: When Nuba falls in battle, she consumes one Source of the Drought Devil to revive at her current location and restore HP.

Self-revive gated behind the Drought Devil sources generated by expiring Doom Marks. Requires investment to Lv4 to activate. Encourages early application of marks so sources are available by the time she might die.
Rite of Ashes Skill 3

Enemies infested with Crimson Doom Mark deal less DMG. Each time Nuba casts her Ultimate, all enemies infested with Crimson Doom Mark permanently lose ATK SPD.

Shares its description with Curse of Decay — likely compounds the same debuff stack, effectively doubling the ATK SPD reduction and DMG reduction layers per Ultimate cast, or grants an additional independent stack.

Recommended Positioning
⚔ PVP — Front Row

Nuba belongs in the front row of PVP compositions. Her ATK SPD drain stacks up while enemies chip away at her, and her self-revive from Drought Devil sources gives her exceptional staying power during the critical opening phases. By the time enemies expect her to be eliminated, she has shredded their attack speed and is still fighting.

🌿 PVE — Front or Mid

In PVE (campaign, bosses), Nuba can anchor the front line while her marks generate Drought Devil sources for the revive. Her Energy drain on the Ultimate active can delay boss ultimate activations, buying time for backline DPS to ramp. She is a utility frontliner rather than a primary carry.

Strengths & Weaknesses
Strengths
  • Applies Crimson Doom Mark at battle start — no ramp-up required for debuffing
  • Permanent stacking ATK SPD reduction on all marked enemies makes her more dangerous the longer a fight lasts
  • Built-in self-revive through Drought Devil sources provides exceptional staying power
  • Energy drain/destruction on Ultimate directly delays and disrupts enemy ultimate rotations
  • Guaranteed Stun on Skill 1 against marked targets — near-100% uptime on CC
  • Interrupt on Skill 1 even without a marked target provides baseline disruption
  • Dual debuff coverage: DMG reduction + ATK SPD reduction simultaneously
Weaknesses
  • Self-revive requires Skill 2 at Lv4 — non-functional at lower investment
  • Revive depends on Drought Devil sources existing — if marks expire before she dies, she may have no revive available
  • Single-target focus on Ultimate active — mark re-application hits only nearest enemy
  • No direct healing outside of the revive HP restore — vulnerable to sustained burst before marks expire
  • Skill 3 (Rite of Ashes) mirrors Skill 2 (Curse of Decay) in text — may indicate a design redundancy or unclear stacking behavior requiring in-game verification
  • Utility-focused — not a standalone damage carry; needs supporting DPS in the backline
Synergy Considerations

Club faction stacking is Nuba's most concrete synergy path. Running 3–5 Club heroes gives the whole team bonus ATK and HP — both stats directly benefit Nuba (more HP means her revive stretches further; more ATK amplifies her Stun windows). A full 5-Club composition gives +35% to the entire lineup, making it a viable dedicated archetype around her.


Mechanically, Nuba pairs best with heroes that slow enemy Energy fill rate — since her Ultimate destroys enemy Energy, any hero that further delays enemies reaching their Ultimate creates a compounding effect where enemy ultimates almost never fire. Similarly, heroes that speed up Nuba's own Energy fill let her cast her Ultimate more often, stacking ATK SPD reductions faster.


In PVP she benefits from a second frontliner who can absorb pressure while her ATK SPD stacks accumulate — ideally another Club Warrior or tank. Backline scaling DPS heroes pair well since Nuba's debuffs buy them the time they need to ramp damage.

Playstyle Summary

Nuba is a slow-burn oppressor. She does not win fights instantly — she wins by making enemies progressively less capable of fighting back. Every Ultimate cast is a permanent tax on the enemy team's speed and damage. Every Skill 1 is a near-guaranteed Stun. Every expiring Doom Mark feeds her revive pool.

She is at her best in prolonged PVP engagements where her stacking debuffs compound, and is a reliable frontline disruptor in PVE where her Energy drain disrupts boss timing. She demands investment (especially Skill 2 to Lv4) but rewards it with outsized survivability and sustained enemy suppression that few Warriors can replicate.

Play her in the front row. Mark everything at the start. Cast your Ultimate as often as possible to stack the ATK SPD drain. Let the Drought Devils accumulate — and treat her revive as a free second life that the enemy didn't expect.
