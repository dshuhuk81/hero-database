The highest difficulty level for the Delusion Nest is currently 6, requiring completion of Chapter 31. This roguelike mode is very interesting.
Passing through the game shouldn't be a problem, but getting a high score and ranking will require some skill in choosing your choices.
1. Starting buff selection: You can choose Masked Man (requires a support in the middle of the back row) or Ripper (increases critical hit damage against the enemy in the first half of the game).
2. Team composition: For best AoE damage, choose: Wind Yi, Triad, Sea God, Nemesis. For single-target damage, Isis is the best. Support options: God of Wealth, God of Wine, Athena.
3: After selecting your team, begin the battle. There's a progress bar at the top of the screen; as you fight monsters, the bar will increase. When it's full, three buff options will appear. Prioritize increasing critical hit rate (critical strike chance), followed by increasing attack damage and gaining energy upon defeating monsters. For buffs, two are sufficient: a shield for finishing moves and healing for finishing moves.
4: The five icons at the top represent the progress of various buffs. A buff will evolve once at level 5, once every 10 levels, and fully evolve at 15 levels. Here are a few fully evolved and useful buffs to choose from: use a finishing move at the start of the battle, and ignore defense when using a finishing move.
5: When clearing the sixth wave of monsters, you will encounter a score bottleneck. You only have 45 seconds to clear the score bottleneck. The more monsters you kill in these 45 seconds, the higher your ranking will be. Therefore, try to maximize your attack and energy in the earlier waves of monsters so that you can kill more monsters and get more points

It's too difficult if your character level is low, especially the final level, Nuwa!
🤔1: Let's talk about the strategy. At the start, choose critical strike. In the first wave, you must collect 3-4 critical strike icons, and they should be those that increase critical strike chance: "95% critical strike chance", "critical strike probability", "critical strike damage", and "critical strike energy regeneration". If you don't get them, log out and start over.
🤔2: Enter the second wave to collect 5 critical hit icons and farm "critical hit ignores 100% defense". Once you get it, farm "kill to restore energy", "ultimate skill to restore energy" and "finishing move to restore energy". This is because we need to consider farming points in the seventh wave, and use the area-of-effect kill skill of the sealing aunt as quickly as possible.
🤔3: Since the third wave cannot be farmed, you will level up after completing it, select the icon, and enter the fourth wave at the same time, so try to farm "critical hit ignore defense" in the second wave.
🤔4: You can also farm in the fourth wave, but logging out requires fast hands and you're too lazy to concentrate. You can farm other things in this wave, such as critical hit, critical hit damage, attack speed, full energy at the start, etc. Players with low character levels can farm basic icons in this wave. Collect 5 icons to get "full energy at the start" to prevent "Pan" from entering and not being able to use skills. You can also farm 10 critical hit icons to get "critical hit 25% HP instant kill".
🤔5: You can also farm in the fifth wave. Remember to switch to "Pan" to farm energy. Once you get "Full Energy at the Start", farm "Energy Recovery on Kill", "Ultimate Skill Damage", "Critical Hit Shield", "Energy Recovery on Kill", and "Ultimate Skill Shield" to defend against Nuwa's minions. Nuwa has a minion that can rush in and kill us instantly.
🤔6: There's nothing to note about the sixth wave. If you encounter any unexpected situations, just log out and start over.
