# Motto Immortal — Beginner Guide: The Why & The How

**Who this guide is for:** New players (Russian server, global newcomers, or anyone starting fresh) who want to progress fast without wasting resources. This guide is written from a **F2P and low-spender perspective**. If you plan to whale, most recommendations still apply — you just have more flexibility with who to build.

**How this guide works:** Every recommendation comes with a short *Why* (so you understand the logic) and a clear *How* (so you know exactly what to do). If you remember only one thing, make it this: **in Motto Immortal, carries are the fixed point and supports rotate around them**. That is the opposite of most gacha games, and it shapes every decision below.

---

## Part 1 — Your First 48 Hours

### 1.1 Day 1 Checklist

**Why:** Your first day sets the trajectory for the next two weeks. A few small mistakes now cost real resources later.

**How — do these in order:**

1. Finish the tutorial and play Odyssey until it starts resisting you (usually around Chapter 5–8).
2. Claim every mail and every login reward — do not let them sit.
3. Open the Shop and buy the **30%-off Normal Pull** in the top-left of the Resource tab. Always. Every day.
4. Open your **Wish List** (see Part 5.1) and pick your free early hero per faction.
5. Spend your first-day gems on pulls. Do **not** hoard on Day 1 — you need bodies on the field to progress. You will start hoarding from Day 3.
6. Before logging off, make sure all daily missions are cleared. Even partial completion gives you diamonds and pull fragments.

### 1.2 The Pull Strategy (Save vs. Spend Timing)

**Why:** Day 1–2 and Day 3+ use two completely different strategies. On Day 1, you have almost no heroes — so pulls directly translate into progression. From Day 3 onward, you have enough of a core to push stages, and every extra pinion is better saved for a rate-up.

**How:**

- **Day 1–2:** Spend freely. The goal is to have enough heroes to field a full team (5 heroes) and clear early Odyssey chapters. Running out of diamonds on Day 2 is fine.
- **Day 3 onward:** Switch to saving mode. Keep pulling on the daily 30%-off Normal Pull, but stop spending diamonds on banners unless it is a rate-up you planned for.
- **Faction pulls (gold pinions):** Save them for faction-specific rate-ups. Do not burn them on the default banner.
- **Starglint pulls (rings):** Starglint heroes are high-impact but copy-hungry. If you are F2P, save rings for known meta picks rather than gambling on new releases.

### 1.3 Shop Priorities

**Why:** The shop is one of the biggest resource multipliers in the game, and most of it is a trap. Most items are not worth it. A few are mandatory.

**How — what to buy, in this exact priority order:**

1. **30%-off Normal Pull** (Resource tab, first slot) — every day, no exceptions.
2. **Entire second row of the Resource tab** — gold, Omen Tome (pull fragments), and resource boxes. All of it.
3. **Daily shop refresh** — refresh up to **3 times per day** (stop at the 500-diamond refresh cost). Only refresh further if you are whaling to rush progress.
4. **Curio tab** — almost everything here is mandatory. The only skip is the **1000-diamond self-select red gear box** — its value-per-diamond is terrible unless you are racing the leaderboards.
5. **Decomposition shop** — buy out all the water droplets first, then anything else is flexible.
6. **Later (once unlocked):** Red and Yellow relic upgrade waters move into the rotating slots. Buy them with gold freely; only spend diamonds on them if you are rushing.

**Do NOT buy:** random gear chests, cosmetic bundles that do not include currency, or anything priced in diamonds that you cannot immediately evaluate in pulls.

## Part 2 — Pushing Odyssey (The Main Quest)

### 2.1 Why Odyssey Matters

**Why:** Odyssey (the main campaign) is the single biggest source of progression rewards in the early game. Every chapter cleared drops **Undying Flame**, which upgrades your **Shrine**. The Shrine gives account-wide stat bonuses to all heroes you own — bonuses that apply in every game mode and never go to waste. Skipping Odyssey to grind side content is the most common beginner mistake.

### 2.2 Key Milestones to Aim For

**How — push toward these chapters in order:**

| Milestone | What it unlocks | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Around Chapter 10 | Most core game modes | You go from "one mode" to "full game" |
| Around Chapter 20 | First awakening reward tier | Awakening is a major power spike per hero |
| Chapter 39 | Mid-game reward tier | Opens up serious account-wide buffs |
| **Chapter 57** | **4th Awakening unlock** | **Currently the highest reward milestone.** After this, the cost-to-reward ratio of pushing further drops sharply until new content is added. |

Once you reach roughly Chapter 57, further pushes are mostly for pride and small relic drops. Focus your attention on Shrine, Beacon Tree, and PvE mode rewards from that point on.

### 2.3 How to Avoid Getting Stuck

**Why:** Players get stuck because they spread gear and resources across too many heroes. A stage wall is almost never a "roster" problem — it is an "investment" problem.

**How:**

- **Gear only your top 2 DPS first.** Do not give a single piece of gear to your supports until your main carries are fully kitted. Supports contribute through skills, not stats.
- **One full team before you build a second.** In the early phase you run one team. Do not split resources into a "PvP team" and "PvE team" until you are comfortably past Chapter 30.
- **Use the Deity Swap Event (Part 6) if you invested in the wrong hero.** It is not a mistake eraser, but it can move stats off a deadweight pick.
- **Hit-checking a wall:** If you cannot clear a stage after three attempts, do not brute-force it. Go check your Shrine level, your Beacon Tree, and your top carry's relic level. The power gap is almost always there, not in the stage itself.

## Part 3 — Key Features & Unlock Order

### 3.1 Shrine — Your Account Power Engine

**Why:** The Shrine is the single most impactful long-term investment. Undying Flame from Odyssey feeds it, and its bonuses scale every hero you own, in every mode.

**How:** Push Odyssey whenever Shrine is your bottleneck. Do not ignore daily Shrine quests. Some heroes (noted in Part 5) double as **Shrine-friendly picks** — meaning they are useful even at low investment because their kit works in the Shrine's placement system.

### 3.2 Torment Rift — The Other Half of Account Power

**Why:** Torment Rift rewards upgrade your **Beacon Tree**, which gives account-wide stat bonuses — the same category of evergreen investment as the Shrine. The **Calamity Tier** rewards are the threshold you are aiming for.

**How:** Do not skip this mode. Even a modest weekly push feeds Beacon Tree upgrades that help everything else. Prioritize heroes with revive or sustained damage kits for Torment Rift.

### 3.3 Delusion's Den and Grim Surge

**Why:** These are specialized modes that reward different kits than Odyssey. They matter, but less than Odyssey, Shrine, and Torment Rift in your first month.

**How:** Clear them weekly at whatever level you can. Do not build heroes specifically for these modes until you have a solid Odyssey core.

### 3.4 What to Ignore Early

- **PvP Arena deep pushes.** Clear your dailies for the rewards, but do not stress about rank in Week 1–2. PvP rewards a wider roster than you have yet.
- **Dedicated second and third teams.** Not yet. One team. See Part 2.3.
- **Self-select red gear boxes at 1000 diamonds.** Already mentioned, worth repeating.

## Part 4 — Pinions (Feathers): Save or Spend?

### 4.1 The Three Pinion Types

**Why:** Different pinions pull from different pools. Using the wrong one at the wrong time is a common F2P trap.

| Pinion Type | Pulls From | When To Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Normal Pinions** (standard feathers) | All non-Starglint heroes | Anytime — the base pool |
| **Gold Pinions** (faction feathers) | One specific faction | **Only on faction rate-ups.** Do not waste on standard banner. |
| **Rings** (Starglint pinions) | Starglint heroes only | Rate-ups for Starglint heroes you already planned to build |

### 4.2 The Golden Rule

**Why:** Pinions never expire in any meaningful window. A pinion spent on a filler banner is a pinion that cannot catch a rate-up next week.

**How:**

- **Day 1–2:** spend almost everything to get a workable roster.
- **From Day 3:** the default answer is **save**, unless a specific rate-up hero is on your plan.
- **Rule of thumb:** never pull on a banner whose featured hero you cannot name from memory. If you are pulling just because pulls are available, stop.

### 4.3 Rate-Up Events to Watch For

**Why:** Rate-ups are the single best value in the game for F2P players. They concentrate your pinions on a hero you actually want, and many of them come with pity discounts or free multi-pulls.

**How:** When you log in, always check the event calendar for **faction rate-ups** and **returning hero banners**. Look for two signals:

1. A hero from your **Wish List** (see Part 5.1) is featured.
2. A **meta carry** (see Part 5.2) is featured with a guaranteed pity within a reasonable pinion budget.

If neither is true, keep saving. Skipping a rate-up is always okay. Burning pinions on a "maybe" is not.

## Part 5 — Which Deities to Focus On (F2P)

**Hero-name caveat:** The specific names below reflect the current meta. Metas shift — when in doubt, cross-check against the site's tier list. The *roles and patterns* below will remain true even if individual names cycle out.

### 5.1 The Wish List — Your Free Early Pick Per Faction

**Why:** The Wish List lets you influence which hero you pull in each faction. Picking wrong here means your gold pinions feed the wrong hero. This is a one-time-per-faction decision that echoes for weeks.

**How — recommended picks by faction (F2P / low-spend):**

- **Hearts:** **Caishen** (the "Fortune" god) — top-tier energy support, slots into almost every team. If you already have Caishen, pick **Moonmatch** (Yue Lao) — the single best four-system support in the game, stacks attack, crit, heal, and energy.
- **Spades:** **Hecate** — the Spade main carry, essential for Delusion's Den and a strong long-term push-chapter DPS. Her relic unlocks her full kit at **Level 10**, so keep that goal in mind.
- **Diamonds:** **Phoenix** — strong early clear tool, mandatory for Torment Rift (infinite revives at higher relic levels), and stays relevant as a 4th- or 5th-team carry. Alternative: **Sekhmet** if you specifically want a single-target Diamond melee carry.
- **Clubs:** **Fengyi** — an all-phase AoE carry who performs well in open-world pushing. Dual pick: **Demeter** for one of the four strongest tanks in the game at base copy.

### 5.2 Core Carries to Build

**Why:** This game treats carries as the fixed center of each team. Supports rotate in and out around them. These are the carries F2P players can realistically invest in and get full value:

- **Heracles** — a Spade bruiser carry who leads a team that is forgiving to build (good pairing with almost any four supports).
- **Nezha** — exceptional carry, but gated behind **Peerless+ rarity** to really shine. Skip unless you can commit the copies.
- **Ice Queen / Meret** — control-focused carry. Her team scales with her own investment more than most.
- **Fengyi** — the Club all-rounder mentioned above.
- **Hecate** — scales from "rough" to "monster" across Relic Level 10.

**Star Card (Starglint) picks for F2P:** Do not chase Zeus unless you can fully star him and push his relic to 30 — his damage ceiling is unreachable otherwise. Instead, get one copy of **Nyx** first (solid early damage, becomes a sub-carry later), then **Nüwa** (tankiest hero in the game when built, also a flex carry), then **Dionysus** (mandatory for damage-check modes and situational push). **Amun** (if available through the story leaderboard) is a free tanky flex — take him whenever offered.

### 5.3 Core Supports to Build

**Why:** Supports in this game are either "build once and use forever" or "need copies." Prioritize the single-copy wonders.

**How — these supports are worth building at one copy:**

- **Caishen** — energy restoration god. Slot and forget.
- **Moonmatch (Yue Lao)** — mentioned above. The best all-in-one support.
- **Demeter** — top-four tank at base copy.
- **Prometheus** — top-four tank, slightly relic-dependent.
- **Nüwa** — top-four tank, scales the most with investment.
- **Momus** — immortal, AoE taunt, self-heal; a top-four Club tank. Shrine-friendly too.
- **Diana** — energy-return utility support; one copy is enough, which frees you to move her to other teams.

### 5.4 Who NOT to Invest In Early

- **Heroes that need multiple copies to function** (any hero whose key breakpoint is at dupe 3 or higher). At 1 copy, they are bait.
- **PvP-only specialists** before you have a PvE core.
- **Old meta carries that recent balance patches have moved on from.** Check the site tier list — if a hero has slipped out of the top tier and is not on a current rate-up, skip.

## Part 6 — The Deity Swap Event: Your Second Chance

### 6.1 What It Is

**Why:** The Deity Swap Event lets you take a hero you have invested in — stats, awakening, everything — and transfer that investment onto a different hero. In a game where early-game mistakes compound, this is a major safety net.

### 6.2 When to Use It

**How:**

- **Do not use it immediately.** Wait until you have enough game knowledge to know which hero you want to swap *into*. Swapping into another bad pick wastes the event.
- **Wait for the swap to open during an event window.** It is not always available.
- **Best targets to swap out:** heroes you built before reading a guide, old-meta carries who fell off (e.g., Ares after his nerf), or duplicates of a role you already have covered.
- **Best targets to swap into:** the F2P core picks from Part 5 — Caishen, Moonmatch, Hecate, Phoenix, or whichever carry is currently your team's bottleneck.

### 6.3 Common Swap Patterns

- **Off a starter carry into a meta carry.** Many players swap their initial leveled hero into Hecate or Fengyi once they understand how the team compositions work.
- **Off an over-invested duplicate support into a tank.** If you built two supports of similar roles, swap one into Demeter, Prometheus, or Nüwa.
- **Off a Starglint experiment.** If you accidentally invested Starglint resources into a hero you no longer want to play, the swap event is your escape.

## Part 7 — Resource Efficiency Rules

These are the rules experienced players take for granted. Internalizing them early is worth more than any single hero.

### 7.1 One Core Team First

Build five heroes to a high level before building anyone else. Splitting into two half-built teams is always worse than one full team and a bench.

### 7.2 Gear: Top 2 DPS Always

Gear rolls are rare. Put every piece on your top two damage dealers until they are fully kitted. Your supports do not need gear to fulfill their role.

### 7.3 Relic Breakpoints That Matter Early

Some heroes only "wake up" at a specific relic level. Hitting that level is usually worth more than hitting +1 on three lesser heroes.

- **Hecate — Relic Level 10** (unlocks her full kit; below this she is mediocre)
- **Poseidon — Relic Level 10** (before this, too weak to justify a slot)
- **Phoenix — higher relic levels** (unlocks infinite revive; Torment Rift standout)

If you own any of the above, push their relic to the breakpoint before spreading relic materials around.

### 7.4 Don't Overlevel in Early Phase

Leveling heroes costs resources you could spend on your core. Keep bench heroes at a level where they can clear their daily content and no higher. Overleveling is the silent F2P killer.

## Part 8 — Common Beginner Mistakes

- **Hoarding from Day 1.** You need bodies. Spend on Day 1–2, then save.
- **Spreading gear across six heroes.** Two DPS get everything.
- **Pulling on every banner "just in case."** If you cannot name the featured hero, you are wasting pinions.
- **Chasing Zeus without the copies.** Without Peerless and Relic 30, he is just an expensive average unit.
- **Ignoring Shrine and Beacon Tree.** These are the only investments that apply to every mode, every hero, forever.
- **Burning the Deity Swap Event on impulse.** Wait. Think. Then swap.
- **Refreshing the shop past 500 diamonds.** Bad value for F2P. Stop at 3 refreshes.
- **Building a second team before the first is finished.** One team to Chapter 30. Then diversify.
- **Ignoring the Wish List.** Your free early picks matter more than most people realize.
