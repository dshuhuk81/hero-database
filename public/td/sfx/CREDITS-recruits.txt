Recruit hero sounds (public/td/sfx), September 28, 2026.

mythic-recruit-*-v4_{attack|ultimate}.ogg are copies of the class donor's mythic
files (tank: heimdall, warrior: helios, assassin: vidar, mage: boreas, archer:
atalanta, support: asclepius), generated by scripts/td-recruit-assets.py. Sources and
licenses: see CREDITS-mythic.txt. Replace with per-recruit sounds in a later
asset pass; levels are in src/data/tdAudioLevels.json.

Recruit art (public/td/heroes-alt/recruit-*) is generated placeholder art:
the game's own class icons (public/td/icons/classes/) on muted class-colored
backgrounds. Swap the files for real art later; ids and stats stay.
