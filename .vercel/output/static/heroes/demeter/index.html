Skills

The Earth’s Vigor

Skill 1

Demeter roots herself, restraining enemies around her for 3s, dealing DMG equal to 100% of ATK every second. Each enemy restores HP equal to 90% of ATK to her per second. Demeter gains Super Armor for the duration.

LvL. 2 Increases DMG per second to 110% of ATK.
LvL. 3 Each enemy restores HP equal to 100% of her ATK per second.
LvL. 4 Increases DMG per second to 120% of ATK.

Sprawling Thorns

Skill 2

Demeter controls vines to restrain the farthest enemy, dealing DMG equal to 270% of ATK and draining 100 Energy from them.

LvL. 2 Increases DMG dealt to 300% of ATK.
LvL. 3 Energy drained increases to 150.
LvL. 4 Increases DMG dealt to 330% of ATK.

Return of the Maiden

Skill 3

Restores 2% HP every 0.2s for 3s when Demeter’s HP drops below 50%. Can be triggered once per battle.

LvL. 2 Restores 3% HP every 0.2s.
LvL. 3 Duration extends to 4s.
LvL. 4 Can trigger twice per battle and once every 6s.

Harvest’s Blessing

Skill 4

Demeter grants a shield equal to 200% of ATK to herself and the most vulnerable ally. The shield lasts up to 10s.

LvL. 2 Shield strength increases to 220% of ATK.
LvL. 3 Increases M-RES by 50% while the shield is active.
LvL. 4 Shield strength increases to 250% of ATK.

Four Seasons’ Bounty

Relic

Demeter enters battle with 3 stacks of Abundance. Each stack reduces DMG taken by 15%. For every 30% of max HP lost, one stack is consumed. Stacks are regained when her HP is restored to a certain level.

LvL. 2 When Demeter has 3 stacks of Abundance, she is immune to Crowd Control.
LvL. 3 Each stack of Abundance reduces DMG taken by 17.5%.
LvL. 4 Each stack of Abundance reduces DMG taken by 20%.
