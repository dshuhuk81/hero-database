Skills

Law of Creation

Skill 1

At combat start, summons a Bulwark Figurine that charges the nearest enemy, dealing DMG equal to 270% of ATK and stunning them for 2s. The Bulwark Figurine inherits 200% of her max HP and 100% of her ATK. Activating the skill deploys a barrier equal to 1600% of ATK for 6s that absorbs damage for allies behind it. If a Bulwark Figurine already exists, its HP is fully restored and the barrier is redeployed.

LvL. 2 The shield absorbs DMG equal to 2200% of ATK.
LvL. 3 The shield absorbs DMG equal to 3000% of ATK.
LvL. 4 While the barrier is active, the Bulwark Figurine takes 20% reduced DMG.

Divine Embrace

Skill 2

Grants a shield equal to 240% of ATK to a frontline ally and all allies within 3 meters. While the shield is active, it blocks one incoming Crowd Control effect.

LvL. 2 Shield strength increases to 270% of ATK.
LvL. 3 Nuwa gains a shield when casting the skill.
LvL. 4 Takes 50% less DMG while shielded.

Children of the Clay

Skill 3

Summons a Greataxe Figurine inheriting 150% of her max HP and 120% of her ATK. It attacks the farthest enemy, dealing DMG equal to 300% of ATK and stunning them for 1s. If a Greataxe Figurine already exists, it is fully healed and leaps into enemy clusters, dealing 300% ATK DMG and knocking enemies down.

LvL. 2 Increases Greataxe Figurine stun duration to 1.5s.
LvL. 3 Leaps high and lands, taunting enemies within range for 5s and gaining 100% Armor for the duration.
LvL. 4 Increases smash DMG and clustered area DMG to 330% of ATK.

Sky Salvation

Skill 4

Summons a Support Figurine inheriting 100% of her max HP and 150% of her ATK. It heals the most vulnerable ally for 100% ATK every 2.5s. If a Support Figurine already exists, it is fully healed and instead heals the most vulnerable ally for 300% ATK.

LvL. 2 Grants buffs based on ally position: frontline allies gain 10% Dodge Rate, backline allies gain 10% DMG bonus for 10s.
LvL. 3 Support Figurine now heals for 110% ATK with each Normal Attack.
LvL. 4 When a Bulwark Figurine is on the battlefield, she heals it every 2.5s.

Primordial Spirit

Relic

Casting her Ultimate instantly triggers Children of the Clay and Sky Salvation once.

LvL. 2 Nuwa gains an additional 30% DMG Reduction for each Figurine on the battlefield.
LvL. 3 Nuwa gains 100 Energy for each Figurine defeated.
LvL. 4 Whenever an ally summons a minion, Nuwa blesses it with Divine Embrace, granting it a shield.
