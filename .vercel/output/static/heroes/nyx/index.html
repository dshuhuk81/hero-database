Skills

Evernight Raid

Skill 1

Nyx blinks behind the symmetrically opposite enemy at combat start, dealing DMG equal to 200% of ATK. She also prevents the target from recovering Energy for 8s and stuns them for 3s.

LvL. 2 Increases DMG dealt to 220%.
LvL. 3 Stuns last for 4s.
LvL. 4 Increases DMG dealt to 250%.

Nocturnal Judgment

Skill 2

Performs a 4-hit combo on the nearest enemy, each hit dealing DMG equal to 180% of ATK. Nyx is untargetable and continuously controls the enemy during this. The final hit stuns the target for 2s.

LvL. 2 The last hit deals bonus DMG equal to 10% of the target's lost HP (up to 200% of Nyx's ATK).
LvL. 3 Increases DMG per hit to 200% ATK.
LvL. 4 The last hit deals bonus DMG equal to 15% of the target's lost HP (up to 300% of Nyx's ATK). (Unlocks at Lv.261)

Encroaching Nightfall

Skill 3

Nyx creates a Dark Domain in an enemy-clustered area and becomes untargetable for the duration. She strikes enemies in Dark Domain 3 times, each dealing DMG equal to 150% of ATK. She emerges in the end and deals DMG equal to 250% of ATK to all enemies in Dark Domain.

LvL. 2 Strikes enemies in Dark Domain 3 times, each dealing DMG equal to 180% of ATK.
LvL. 3 Increases the final hit's DMG to 280%.
LvL. 4 The final hit is guaranteed to be a CRIT hit.

Domain of Night

Skill 4

For each surviving ally (excluding minions) on the battlefield, Nyx gains 5% Dodge Rate and a 4% ATK boost.

LvL. 2 For each surviving ally on the field (excluding summons), Nyx gains 10% Dodge Rate and a 5% ATK boost.
LvL. 3 During the first 10s of the battle, this skill's bonus is doubled.
LvL. 4 For each surviving ally on the field (excluding summons), Nyx gains 20% Dodge Rate and a 6% ATK boost. (Unlocks at Lv.281)

Darkest Fear

Relic

If a target marked by Evernight Raid is defeated, applies Terror to enemies near the target for 2s.

LvL. 2 Enemies damaged by Evernight Raid cannot receive shields or healing. (Unlock at Relic Lv.10)
LvL. 3 If a target marked by Evernight Raid is defeated, applies Terror to enemies near the target for 3s. (Unlock at Relic Lv.20)
LvL. 4 All DMG dealt by Nyx ignores 70% of the target's Armor. (Unlock at Relic Lv.30)
