Beginner Guide

Everything you need to know to start strong in Motto Immortal

📋 What's in This Guide

⚔️ Best Early Game Heroes 🔮 Relic Priority ♈ Zodiac Selection 🏆 Normal Arena Teams ⚡ Triple Duel (Advanced)

What This Guide Covers

This guide is built for new players who want a strong foundation without wasting rare resources. Everything here is based on game mechanics: items, stats, skills, relic breakpoints, suit advantage, and why certain heroes scale better in the early-to-mid game.

Progression Phases

  • Early Game: 1 main team (5 heroes) that clears Odyssey + performs in Torment Rift.
  • Mid Game: 2–3 teams (you start specializing: PvE bosses / Odyssey / PvP).
  • Endgame: 4–5 optimized teams, relic breakpoints, bonds, and event-driven upgrades.

Key idea: build a strong PvE core first. PvE unlocks the resources that make everything else easier.

Early Game Priorities (Do This First)

1) Push Odyssey (Campaign) for Undying Flame

Undying Flame is the most important early progression item because it upgrades your Shrine. Shrine upgrades increase long-term account power and your ability to farm better gear.

2) Compete in Torment Rift for Calamity Tier

The second most valuable early item is Calamity Tier (Torment Rift rewards). It upgrades your Beacon Tree which grants account-wide stat bonuses to all heroes.

Why Beacon Tree matters

Beacon bonuses apply in all game modes. Even if the meta changes, Beacon investment stays valuable. This is one of the best “never wasted” progress systems for F2P / low spenders.

3) Get Creation Essence (Beacon Tree Upgrades)

Creation Essence is used to level the Beacon Tree. When upgraded, it strengthens even low-copy heroes because the bonus is shared across your whole roster.

1) Most Valuable Heroes in the Early Game (And Why)

The “Versatile PvE Core” (Recommended First Investments)

If you are F2P / low spender, prioritize heroes that perform well in multiple modes: Odyssey, Boss Hunts (Torment Rift), and Spire. These carry your account progression.

Basic hero recommendations

Fengyi • Poseidon • Bastet • Mumus • Hela • Nemesis

Why these heroes?

  • Fengyi: Fengyi is one of the most efficient early investments in the game. A Divine Fangi with a Level 30 relic can dominate PvP and performs well across nearly all game modes. Build this hero early and you will maintain a strong position throughout your progression.
  • Poseidon: Poseidon is a frontliner with one of the best ultimate skill timings in the game — he casts his AOE crowd control within the first few seconds of battle. This early cast can decide the outcome of fights before they even develop. He also provides a large HP boost to the team on cast. Best-in-slot front line for most beginner and mid-game teams.
  • Bastet:Bastet targets high-damage enemy units and has a dodge mechanic that makes her incredibly hard to punish. Every dodge she performs also deals additional damage back. She is especially strong against carry-heavy enemy teams.
  • Numus: Mumus brings a Taunt mechanic that forces enemies to target her, protecting your key damage dealers. She pairs exceptionally well with units like Bastet and Isis. A great support investment that immediately improves your team's survivability.
  • Hela: Hela is a burst damage dealer with enormous late-fight payoff. When paired with Yuelao and given a few seconds to survive, she can obliterate entire enemy teams. She requires some investment to reach her potential but is a worthwhile early pickup.
  • Nemesis: Nemesis becomes a dominant force at max relic awakening. She is a strong early-game pick that keeps improving as you invest in her relic — making her a great long-term project you can start immediately.

High-Value Early Core (Better heroes)

Isis • Hladgunnr • Caishen • Yuelao • Dionysus

Why these heroes?

  • Isis: Core PvE damage dealer that scales hard with gear investment. Strong in bosses, Spire, and progression.
  • Hladgunnr: Consistent damage and very strong PvE coverage; works across multiple modes.
  • Caishen: Improves team rotation reliability (energy economy). Rotation stability is often the difference between clearing and failing.
  • Yuelao: Utility/support that helps consistent performance across game modes.
  • Dionysus: Key synergy enabler: provides ATK SPD which massively increases the output of certain carries (notably Isis).

Why this one? (Example: Isis + Dionysus)

Isis is a carry that benefits heavily from ATK SPD. Dionysus provides ATK SPD, meaning Isis triggers more damage events and cycles faster. This increases overall damage and consistency in both PvE and PvP.

🌟 Star Heroes — Who is Worth It at 1 Copy?

Good at base copy (Legendary): Nuwa, Amun Ra, Nyx, Dionysus — these provide immediate value and don't require heavy investment to contribute.

Require heavy investment: Zeus, Nezha — hold off on these until you have the resources to fully build them. They underperform at low investment.

Early Game “Rules” That Decide Fights

  • Do not level multiple heroes at once. Pick 5–6 core heroes and max them before touching others. A fully-built Fangi or Poseidon beats a half-built team of ten heroes every time.
  • Suit advantage: always check the enemy suit and match the advantage (example: Hearts deal more damage vs Spades).
  • Link skills: build teams where skills amplify each other (not five random strong units).
  • Level thresholds matter: reaching breakpoints (like unlocking new passives via resonance level) can spike power.
  • Gear priority: put best gear first on your top two PvE damage dealers (commonly Isis + Hladgunnr).

Totems & Suit Advantage (Two Early Game Multipliers)

Suit Advantage: Always Check It

In bosses and many PvE fights, suit advantage is a straightforward damage multiplier. If the boss is Spades, use Hearts to deal more damage and progress faster.

Totems: Choose Based on Your Team Composition

  • Keystone of Wonders: only when your team has many different suits. It scales with “suits owned” and becomes better when your roster is diverse.
  • Horn of Potency: a safe early default when you have only 1–2 suits in your team (simple, consistent team-wide ATK boost).

2) Which Relics Should Be Upgraded First (And Why)

Relic Breakpoints: Don’t Upgrade Randomly

Relics have major breakpoints. Some heroes are already usable with relic unactivated, while others only become “real” heroes at certain relic levels. As F2P / low spender, your goal is to hit the minimum effective breakpoint first.

Relic Philosophy

Upgrade relics for heroes that help you place higher in Torment Rift and clear Odyssey. Better ranks = better rewards = faster account growth.

Priority Order (Practical Early Game)

  1. Boss Hunt / PvE Core Carry Relics (first): your main PvE damage dealers and key supports used in Torment Rift.
    Example from the relic guide: Isis is recommended to reach Relic Level 20 early for full usability.
  2. Key PvP/PvE timing relics (second): heroes whose relic changes the opening seconds of battle.
    Example: Poseidon is valuable around Relic Level 10 because it enables early casting patterns that can swing fights.
  3. Premium / endgame relic investments (later): pushing relics toward level 30 and beyond becomes expensive. Do it when your income supports it.

Suggested Early Relic Targets (From the transcript’s guide)

  • Isis: aim toward Relic 20 early (core PvE investment).
  • Poseidon: strong at Relic 10 due to early fight impact (PvP + certain PvE).
  • Tefnut: recommended to become “fully usable” around Relic 20.
  • High-end celestial units: often scale best at Relic 30 (resource-heavy, do later unless you’re funded).

Resource Reminder (F2P Reality)

Don’t force ultra-rare relic materials early. Focus on the relic materials you can consistently earn via Torment Rift rankings, milestones, and events.

3) Which Zodiac Should Beginners Choose at the Start?

The Rule: Choose Based on Bonds First

The best Zodiac for a beginner depends on your Bond bonuses. Bonds can grant stats like HP +5%, ATK +5%, or starting Energy +200. Before switching, check which bonds you can maximize.

Important: Switching costs gems

  • Switching Zodiac can cost around 999 gems each time.
  • So pick a Zodiac you can commit to early unless you have a plan.

Recommended Beginner Picks (Utility-Based)

  • Gemini (recommended default): has an AoE leap that deals damage and provides a stun. Great for stage clearing and usable in PvP.
  • Pisces (control option): has vortex/pull control and can gain additional stuns as relic levels increase. Great when you need grouping + CC.
  • Capricorn (tactical option): valued for “swap/pull” style utility in difficult stages (helps remove backline threats).

What makes the Zodiac-selected hero different compared to other purple heroes?

  • It’s flexible: you can switch between Zodiac forms (Gemini/Pisces/etc.) depending on what you need.
  • Relic upgrades carry over: if you upgrade the Zodiac relic, that relic level is inherited when you switch forms. This makes Zodiac relic investment uniquely efficient.
  • Bonds add extra stats: when you activate Bond gifts for multiple Zodiac forms, you gain additional stats and utility.

Why this one? (Simple beginner answer)

Choose the Zodiac whose Bond bonus you can realistically activate and whose skill solves your current problem: Gemini for general AoE stun value, Pisces for pull/CC setups, Capricorn for tactical repositioning.

PvP Team – Normal Arena

Best Reference Setup (Beginner-Friendly) and Why

Your Arena goal early is not “perfect meta.” It’s consistent wins and stable defense while you continue building PvE income.

Reference Setup (balanced + accessible)

Mumus (Frontline / Taunt) • Poseidon (Early CC) • Dionysus (ATK SPD) • Isis (Carry DPS) • Caishen (Rotation stability)

Why it’s chosen

  • Mumus: buys time (taunt + frontline stability).
  • Poseidon: early-cast CC can decide fights before the enemy stabilizes.
  • Dionysus: ATK SPD increases carry output and helps your team snowball.
  • Isis: carry DPS that scales with investment (your gear doesn’t get “wasted” later).
  • Caishen: improves rotations and makes your plan consistent instead of RNG-heavy.

Hero Synergy Explanation (Clear & Concrete)

  • Dionysus → Isis: ATK SPD increases Isis’ damage throughput.
  • Mumus → Backline: taunt reduces pressure on your carry/supports.
  • Poseidon → Team: early disruption gives your carry time to ramp and cast.
  • Caishen → Rotations: more consistent energy economy = more consistent ult cycles.

Common Arena mistakes

  • Building only DPS and no frontline (you lose before your carry can cast).
  • Ignoring early-cast control (you get snowballed).
  • Over-investing in PvP early (PvE income is still the real growth engine).

Suggested Lineups: Beginner, Midgame, Endgame

Beginner (1 main team)

Beginner Core

Isis • Hladgunnr • Caishen • Yuelao • Dionysus

  • Optimized for Odyssey progression + Torment Rift ranking.
  • Prioritize gear on Isis and Hladgunnr.

Midgame (2–3 teams start)

Split your roster by purpose:

  • Team 1 (PvE Boss): Carry + rotation support + survivability
  • Team 2 (Odyssey): control/pull tools to remove backline threats
  • Team 3 (PvP): early CC + anti-carry pressure

Midgame Example (PvE Boss Team)

Isis • Hladgunnr • Dionysus • Caishen • Tefnut

Endgame (4–5 teams, premium units)

Endgame teams depend on your celestial pulls and relic economy. In PvP references, top-tier celestial heroes commonly include units like Zeus, Nyx, Dionysus, Nuwa, and Amun Ra, while powerful non-celestial pieces like Poseidon, Bastet, and Hladgunnr remain relevant.

Endgame Example (Arena Pressure)

Poseidon • Bastet • Mumus • Dionysus • (Celestial Carry Slot)

PvP Team – Triple Duel (Advanced Arena)

How to Think About Triple Duel

  • It’s a drafting problem: you need 3 functional teams, not 1 perfect team.
  • Don’t spread your best items evenly. Put your best gear where it wins the hardest matchups.
  • Build one “anchor team” that is the most reliable win condition.

Beginner Triple Duel Plan (Simple)

  1. Team A (Anchor): Your strongest PvE carry team adapted for PvP.
  2. Team B (Control): CC + disruption to steal wins.
  3. Team C (Stall): frontline + survivability to outlast weak opponents.

Recommended Setups (generic templates)

Team A (Anchor): Mumus • Dionysus • Isis • Caishen • Poseidon

Team B (Control): Khepri/Prometheus • Pisces/Gemini Zodiac • Poseidon • Support • DPS

Team C (Stall): Tanks/Immortality tools • shields/heals • one consistent DPS

Tips That Win Matches

  • Know enemy threats: certain backliners can wipe teams if left alone—use pull/swap tools when needed.
  • Position matters: protect your key carry; don’t waste protection on heroes that naturally move/free-cast.
  • Use Totems correctly: Keystone of Wonders is best when your team has many suits; otherwise default to stable ATK options.

Beginner Checklist (Fast)

  • Push Odyssey to farm Undying Flame → upgrade Shrine.
  • Compete in Torment Rift for Calamity Tier → upgrade Beacon Tree.
  • Invest gear first into Isis + Hladgunnr (your PvE engines).
  • Build synergy, not random power: Dionysus supports carries that scale with ATK SPD.
  • Upgrade relics to breakpoints (don’t max everything): Isis ~20, Poseidon ~10, Tefnut ~20 as a practical track.
  • Pick Zodiac based on Bonds + utility: Gemini (stun) / Pisces (pull+CC) / Capricorn (tactical).
