Beginner Guide

Everything you need to know to start strong in Motto Immortal

📋 What's in This Guide

⚔️ Best Early Game Heroes 🔮 Relic Priority ♈ Zodiac Selection 🏆 Normal Arena Teams ⚡ Triple Duel (Advanced)

⚔️ Best Early Game Heroes — and Why

In Motto Immortal, your first investment decisions define your entire progression path. Spreading resources too thin will stall you. Focus on these heroes first.

🥇 Top Priority Heroes

Fangi Early Meta Pick

Fangi is one of the most efficient early investments in the game. A Divine Fangi with a Level 30 relic can dominate PvP and performs well across nearly all game modes. Build this hero early and you will maintain a strong position throughout your progression.

Poseidon S-Tier Tank

Poseidon is a frontliner with one of the best ultimate skill timings in the game — he casts his AOE crowd control within the first few seconds of battle. This early cast can decide the outcome of fights before they even develop. He also provides a large HP boost to the team on cast. Best-in-slot front line for most beginner and mid-game teams.

Bastet Top DPS / Dodge

Bastet targets high-damage enemy units and has a dodge mechanic that makes her incredibly hard to punish. Every dodge she performs also deals additional damage back. She is especially strong against carry-heavy enemy teams.

Mumus Support / Taunt

Mumus brings a Taunt mechanic that forces enemies to target her, protecting your key damage dealers. She pairs exceptionally well with units like Bastet and Isis. A great support investment that immediately improves your team's survivability.

Hela High Burst Potential

Hela is a burst damage dealer with enormous late-fight payoff. When paired with Yuelao and given a few seconds to survive, she can obliterate entire enemy teams. She requires some investment to reach her potential but is a worthwhile early pickup.

Nemesis Scales with Awakening

Nemesis becomes a dominant force at max relic awakening. She is a strong early-game pick that keeps improving as you invest in her relic — making her a great long-term project you can start immediately.

🌟 Star Heroes — Who is Worth It at 1 Copy?

Good at base copy (Legendary): Nuwa, Amun Ra, Nyx, Dionysus — these provide immediate value and don't require heavy investment to contribute.

Require heavy investment: Zeus, Nezha — hold off on these until you have the resources to fully build them. They underperform at low investment.

⚠️ Common Mistake

Do not level multiple heroes at once. Pick 5–6 core heroes and max them before touching others. A fully-built Fangi or Poseidon beats a half-built team of ten heroes every time.

🔮 Relic Priority — What to Upgrade First

Relics are one of the biggest power multipliers in Motto Immortal. Upgrading the wrong relics early is a costly mistake. Follow this priority order to maximize your efficiency.

Priority Tier 1 — Upgrade These First

Fangi's Relic Highest Priority

Fangi's relic, once maxed to Level 30, transforms her into a game-defining force in PvP and PvE alike. This is your first major relic investment goal. If you commit to Fangi early, prioritize her relic above everything else.

Nemesis's Relic Max Awakening Target

Nemesis's power is directly tied to her relic awakening level. At max awakening she is described as "insane" — meaning this relic has a very high ceiling. Invest here early for a hero that keeps scaling.

Priority Tier 2 — Build After Core Units

Bastet's Relic

Once your Bastet is your primary DPS, upgrading her relic increases her dodge frequency and damage burst. Build this after you have her fully ascended.

Poseidon's Relic

Improves his crowd control potency and HP scaling. Good second tank investment.

⚠️ What to Avoid

Do not invest in Athena's relic before building your primary damage dealers. Athena is a powerful support, but only reaches her full potential once you have protected units worth shielding (like Bastet or Poseidon). Building Athena first wastes resources that should go into your carry heroes.

💡 General Rule: Upgrade the relic of whichever hero you use in the most game modes. Fangi qualifies in almost every mode — which is why she consistently tops the relic priority list.

♈ Zodiac Selection — Which Should Beginners Choose?

At the start of the game, you are asked to choose a Zodiac Sign. This is a permanent decision that comes with a free Purple (Epic) hero — so choose wisely.

✅ Recommended Choice: Capricorn

Why Capricorn?

Distinct Capricorn comes with a unique Hero Swap skill — the ability to switch out a hero mid-combat. This provides enormous tactical flexibility, especially in PvP where adapting to enemy lineups matters. Among all the Zodiac options, this mechanic stands out as the most impactful for beginners who are still learning the game's combat system.

What Makes the Zodiac Hero Different from Other Purple Heroes?

Standard Epic (Purple) heroes are either offensive, defensive, or support with fixed kits. The Zodiac heroes — called "Distinct" heroes — each carry a unique passive or active mechanic tied to their constellation that normal Epic heroes do not have:

  • Distinct Cancer: Improves defensive stats of your full team
  • Distinct Gemini: Has a reliable Stun ability
  • Distinct Capricorn: Hero Swap skill — tactical mid-fight flexibility
  • Distinct Aquarius: Good utility and early-game damage
  • Distinct Virgo: Decent mid-game support in niche scenarios

These Distinct heroes are viable all the way through mid-game and in case-specific scenarios beyond that — making your starting Zodiac pick a genuine power spike for your first few weeks.

💡 Tip: Beginners up through mid-game can reliably use Distinct heroes as a consistent team member. Don't dismiss them just because they are Epic rarity — their unique mechanics punch well above their tier.

🏆 PvP Team — Normal Arena

The Normal Arena rewards consistent team compositions over flashy mechanics. Your goal is a durable, synergistic lineup that can auto-battle reliably against a wide range of enemies.

🎯 Best Reference Setup (Poseidon Core)

Poseidon • Mumus • Bastet • Fangi • Nemesis

This lineup combines early crowd control (Poseidon), aggro management (Mumus), high burst DPS (Bastet + Fangi), and scaling damage (Nemesis). It performs reliably in both offensive and defensive arena slots.

💡 Why This Lineup Works — Hero Synergy

  • Poseidon opens the fight with an AOE CC that slows the enemy team — giving your DPS heroes a safe window to burst.
  • Mumus draws enemy attention with Taunt, protecting Bastet and Fangi from being focused down before they can deal damage.
  • Bastet capitalizes on the Mumus taunt by targeting the enemy's strongest damage dealer. Her dodge keeps her alive even when she's eventually targeted.
  • Fangi provides cross-mode reliability. In arena specifically, a Divine Fangi with Level 30 relic is a consistent carry that wins trades.
  • Nemesis ramps up in longer fights where the relic awakening kicks in — she acts as a threat that enemies must deal with or face growing damage.

📈 Suggested Lineups by Progress Stage

🟢 Beginner

Poseidon • Mumus • Fangi • Distinct Capricorn • Nemesis

Use your Zodiac hero (Capricorn) in the flex slot. The Hero Swap skill gives you adaptability when you don't yet have a fifth strong carry. Poseidon + Mumus hold the front, Fangi and Nemesis deal damage.

🔵 Mid-Game

Poseidon • Mumus • Bastet • Fangi • Hela

Introduce Bastet once she is properly invested. Hela joins as a late-fight cleaner — when paired with Yuelao she obliterates teams. If you have Yuelao: Poseidon • Mumus • Bastet • Fangi • Hela + Yuelao support.

🟣 End-Game

Poseidon • Mumus • Bastet • Fangi • Nyx / Dionysus

At end-game, a Star Hero (Nyx or Dionysus) replaces the flex carry slot. Dionysus pairs especially well with Isis if you develop an Isis team — the combination of their skills with a Caishen support is one of the highest-ceiling compositions in the game. Nyx is the go-to assassin carry if you pull her early.

⚠️ Counter Awareness

Anubis hard-counters mage and archer-heavy teams (low HP heroes). If you face Anubis frequently in Arena, ensure you have at least one high-HP frontliner absorbing hits before your squishier carries can act.

⚡ Triple Duel — Advanced Arena Tips

The Triple Duel (also called "Three Kingdoms" or Advanced Arena) requires you to field three separate teams. This is where depth of roster matters — but there are smart ways to approach it even as a beginner.

📌 Core Principle: Specialization per Team

Each team in Triple Duel should serve a distinct role. A mixed team of mediocre heroes across all three slots loses to a player who has one excellent team and two decent ones. Build depth strategically.

🗂️ Recommended Team Distribution

Team 1 — Your Best Team (Primary Carry)

Poseidon • Mumus • Bastet • Fangi • Nemesis

Your main PvP lineup. This should be your most-invested team going into Triple Duel.

Team 2 — Anti-Meta / Counter Team

Anubis • [Crowd Control unit] • DPS • Support • Tank

Anubis is your secret weapon in Triple Duel. He shuts down mage and archer compositions completely — when enemies run glass-cannon carries, Anubis deletes them. Pair him with a CC hero like Distinct Gemini (stun) or Horos (airborne immunity) for maximum disruption.

Team 3 — Stall / Sustain Team

Aris • Prometheus • Tank • Healer • Support

Aris and Prometheus both have immunity-style stalling mechanics. This team's goal is not necessarily to win, but to time-out strong enemy teams and prevent full clears. Prometheus excels in PvE stall and works well in this role. Aris is particularly effective in Shrine-mode and as a defensive anchor.

💡 Triple Duel Tips

  • Scout before assigning: Look at which enemy team is weakest and send your counter team against it — don't auto-assign.
  • Horos counter-pick: When Horos jumps into the air, he becomes immune to all damage. Position Horos on Team 2 as a surprise counter to burst-heavy lineups.
  • Fang is multi-mode: Fangi performs in Triple Duel just as well as in Normal Arena. A Divine Fangi is the best cross-format investment you can make.
  • Avoid Artemis vs. Isis matchups: Artemis always loses to Isis in direct confrontation. If you run Artemis, keep her away from Isis-centric enemy teams.
  • Panic pick: Hela + Yuelao: If you need a third team quickly and are mid-game, Hela + Yuelao as a duo carry with some frontline can win fights by sheer burst potential if Hela survives the opening.

🎯 Long-Term Goal: Build toward having 3 distinct, fully-invested teams. This is the mid-game transition point at around Week 3+. Until then, focus on your primary carry team and use filler for slots 2 and 3 — you'll still earn rewards.

🌟 Quick Reference — Beginner Checklist

  • ✅ Choose Distinct Capricorn as your starting Zodiac for the Hero Swap skill
  • ✅ Build Fangi first — she works in every game mode
  • ✅ Max Fangi's relic to Level 30 as your first major relic goal
  • ✅ Poseidon + Mumus is your best early front line
  • ✅ Don't invest in Athena's relic before your carries are built
  • ✅ In Triple Duel, use Anubis to counter mage/archer teams
  • ✅ Avoid spreading upgrades across too many heroes — focus 5-6 core units first
  • ✅ Join a guild early — progression benefits are significant
