Hero Upgrades

Hero Upgrade System

Understanding the hero upgrade system is crucial for maximizing your team's potential. This guide covers all upgrade paths, resource requirements, and strategic priorities for developing your heroes.

Upgrade Types Overview

Heroes can be upgraded through multiple systems, each providing different benefits:

Upgrade Type Resources Required Main Benefits Priority
Level Up Gold, EXP Potions Base stat increases (HP, ATK, DEF) High
Star Upgrade Hero Shards, Gold Major stat boost, skill unlocks High
Skill Enhancement Skill Books, Gold Increased skill damage/effects Medium
Relic Upgrade Relic Stones, Gold Unique passive bonuses Medium
Gear Enhancement Enhancement Stones, Gold Significant stat increases High
Awakening Awakening Stones, Hero Copies Tier upgrade, massive stat boost Critical
Transcendence Transcendence Stones, Legendary Shards End-game power spike Low (Late Game)

Star Level Requirements

Upgrading hero stars requires specific amounts of hero shards. Plan your resource spending accordingly:

Star Level Common Heroes Epic Heroes Legendary Heroes Unlocks
⭐ 1 Star Base Base Base Hero acquired
⭐⭐ 2 Stars 10 shards 20 shards 30 shards Skill 2 unlocked
⭐⭐⭐ 3 Stars 20 shards 40 shards 60 shards Skill 3 unlocked
⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4 Stars 40 shards 80 shards 120 shards Relic unlocked
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5 Stars 80 shards 160 shards 240 shards Full skill enhancements
6 Stars 160 shards 320 shards 480 shards Awakening available

Gear Enhancement Levels

Each piece of gear can be enhanced to increase its stats. Higher enhancement levels provide exponential benefits:

Enhancement Level Stat Bonus Success Rate Stones Required
+0 to +5 +5% per level 100% Basic Stones
+5 to +10 +8% per level 80% Intermediate Stones
+10 to +15 +12% per level 60% Advanced Stones
+15 to +20 +15% per level 40% Superior Stones
+20 to +25 +20% per level 20% Legendary Stones

Upgrade Priority Guide

Phase 1: Early Game (First 2 Weeks)

  1. Level Up: Keep your main team at max level for your account
  2. Star Upgrades: Focus on getting core heroes to 3-4 stars
  3. Basic Gear: Equip and enhance to +5 for all main heroes

Phase 2: Mid Game (Weeks 3-8)

  1. Star Upgrades: Push main DPS and tank to 5-6 stars
  2. Gear Enhancement: Enhance key pieces to +10
  3. Skill Enhancement: Begin upgrading ultimate skills
  4. Relic Upgrade: Unlock and level up relics for main team

Phase 3: Late Game (Week 8+)

  1. Awakening: Awaken your core legendary heroes
  2. Gear Enhancement: Push to +15 on main team
  3. Full Skill Enhancement: Max out all skills on main heroes
  4. Transcendence: Begin transcending legendary heroes

Resource Management Tips

  • Don't level heroes evenly: Focus resources on your main 5-6 heroes
  • Save legendary shards: Only use them on confirmed end-game heroes
  • Gear enhancement can fail: Use protection scrolls for high-level enhancements (+15 and above)
  • Skill books are limited: Prioritize ultimate skills and key abilities
  • Awakening is expensive: Plan carefully before committing resources

Pro Tips

  • Events often provide discounted upgrade costs - wait for these to maximize efficiency
  • A fully upgraded Epic hero is often better than an under-upgraded Legendary
  • Don't neglect support heroes - their upgrades can win fights
  • Save enhancement protection items for gear pieces with perfect stats
  • Track your upgrade materials - know what you need before events start
  • Consider faction bonuses when deciding which heroes to fully upgrade
