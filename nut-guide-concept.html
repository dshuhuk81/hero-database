Trade-offs

Where she wins, where she dies

Strengths

  • Self-sufficient energy: ult-ready in ~2.5–3s of peace — no battery hero required
  • Interruption only discounts her ult; full denial is nearly impossible
  • Self-ramping ATK: every orbiting core stacks +30% onto her own detonation
  • Layered defense: opening shield → Astral Repulse knockback + 3s untargetable (one proc) → 50% dodge while charging
  • Proven underdog: Arena win at −46% might via one 5-kill detonation

Weaknesses

  • No team utility — her ramp buffs only herself, and her escapes don't protect anyone else: in both recorded losses she was alive and charging while her team died
  • Single-target output is poor — one periodic overkill nuke, weak filler; outdamaged ~2× by a D-rated hero in a one-hitbox room TESTED
  • Her self-ramp paints her own target: ATK stacking makes her the unit that highest-ATK-lock assassins (Horus / Bastet archetype) select CN-COMMUNITY
  • Hard CC before the charge state is her real kill window; only Control-Res answers it
  • Double-assassin dives beat her comp in 15–22s: the knockback+untargetable is one 3s proc on a 14s CD, so the second diver — or the first, 3s later — connects freely. Ranged ATK-lock never triggers it at all
  • Ceiling is relic-gated: untargetable at R10, 1,000-cap at R30, CN endgame expects 30–50 — long investment curve

The reference build's 0% Crit DMG is the loudest gap: her output is one enormous hit, so every point of crit damage multiplies the entire detonation. Defensive substats stay low-priority with one exception — Control-Res. Virtues · pick 3

Protect the ramp, multiply the hit

🛡️

Oblivion

The anti-counter pick

2pc: Armor +30%, M-RES +30% at start · 4pc: CC immunity for 8s at combat start. Eight seconds covers her entire first cycle (~2.5s bank + 5s charge) — it deletes her only true kill window. Mandatory in PvP.

⚡

Fervor

The cycle compounder

2pc: Energy Regen +10% · 4pc: +10% ATK per Ultimate used, max 4 stacks. Amplifies her 200/s engine and snowballs across cycles — at 4 ults per 90s, the full stack arrives in long fights.

🎯

Insight

The opening-crit gamble

2pc: CRIT Rate +10% · 4pc: +30% CRIT Rate before first dealing damage. She deals no damage while channeling — so the bonus may ride into her first Starfall (~57% crit at reference stats). TEST: knockback proc may consume it

💨

Gnosis

Situational swap — not core

2pc: Dodge +10% at start · 4pc: +15% Dodge per hit taken, stacks clear on dodge. Stacks with her 50% charge dodge into near-unhittability vs attack pressure — but dodge stops hits only; CC needs Control-Res. Swap in for Insight against auto-attack-heavy dive comps; dead in PvE.

Alternative for slow PvP matches: Desire (+10% ATK start, +20% after 15s — the Arena fight ran ~23s, so it procs before the decisive detonation; useless in fast PvE clears).

Balanced Mage, or playable astronomical event?

CN calls her "The Nuke" and rates her T0, and the evidence agrees — with one law governing everything: her damage scales linearly with bodies in the circle. 371 billion on an adds-only boss, 20× her teammates in Arena teamfights, and outdamaged by a D-tier hero the moment a room has one hitbox. She loses in 20 seconds to ATK-lock dives her own ramp attracts, and wins at huge might deficits behind a stall package. Don't memorize her mode list — ask her one question before every deploy: how many things are standing in the circle?
